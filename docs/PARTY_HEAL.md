# Stationary party Heal

Party Heal is optional and off by default. Enable it in Recovery for a requested field run, choose a learned or granted Heal level (1–10), an HP threshold, an absolute own-SP reserve, a cooldown (1–3600 seconds), and a finite dispatch allowance (1–100 attempts per run). Existing recovery, item, self/enemy skill, equipment and allocation rules keep their priority. The policy never moves toward a member or interrupts an owned normal attack or walk. Party follow owns preparation, travel and leader reacquisition before Heal; canceled rendezvous movement must also settle.

A candidate must be a visible living player other than self, with a current same-map full party-row binding and fresh shared HP evidence. Party entity zero is unavailable/offline; ordinary own actor zero remains valid. Missing, stale, conflicting, duplicate-ID or replaced-lifetime evidence is unavailable. Full roster evidence must establish membership; names, HP deltas and map-only rows cannot bind an actor. Detached leader-arrival eligibility belongs only to following and cannot grant Heal or shared party resources. Eligible members are ranked by exact HP ratio and then stable member ID. Only candidates within the current field policy and verified stationary range/projectile sight can be cast on; an unreachable candidate is skipped without planning a route.

The client rechecks Heal permission, known body-state prerequisites, observed own casts, standing posture, motion and every outstanding resource/equipment owner before sending. Heal has magic range 9, reduced to 5 by verified Blind. Unknown Blind or disabling-status evidence waits; party support uses the shared engine cast fence, including ambiguous triggered results and retained stationary input cooldown. Estimated cast deadlines and ExtendCast do not release it. An authoritative own CastStop, matching unambiguous execution, CounterAttack completion, actual accepted Walk, or eligible non-center Look may restore availability; physical movement and possibly sent Look delay must still settle. Availability never confirms a Heal receipt or its SP readback. Own SP must be fresh shared observation evidence and agree with character state. Effective cost uses the pinned SP table and verified equipped item/card/refinement/combo metadata with server integer arithmetic; unknown or negative-cost policy data cannot imply affordability. Cost plus reserve must fit current own SP. No gemstone cost is invented.

An attempt and its scheduler sequence, own and target world/incarnation, and party member binding are captured before transport. A send exception, rejection, timeout, Stop, manual game input, actor/member loss, death, map change or disconnect cannot erase that transmitted owner or replay it. The field run retains intent while waiting. Stop and a later Start cannot override an unresolved Heal. A current-lifetime exact late receipt can settle the old owner without starting a new run; a reused actor ID cannot. A new explicit run resets the finite allowance only after prior ownership is settled; the prior cooldown and fresh-SP readback requirement still apply. Temporary pauses and reconnect preserve the current allowance.

Execution confirmation requires a direct targeted skill 41 at the captured level, from own actor to the captured target, result Heal (2), hits 0, and no conflicting separate attacker. The wire's -1 attacker sentinel, omitted attacker or own attacker is accepted. Cast start, indirect impact, HP gain, SP debit, a wrong actor/level/result or a separate attacker does not confirm Heal. Active and canceled receipts use the same matcher. The scheduler waits at least one second after confirmation even when the result's motion is zero. Cooldown also applies. Before another Heal the policy requires a fresh own-SP packet or complete own stats containing SP, received after that exact confirmation for the captured own lifetime. The pinned server emits skill execution before SP debit; a pre-result refresh cannot authorize another Heal. The policy tracks packet order independently of milliseconds, so a result and subsequent SP packet at the same clock time remain ordered. Missing SP, party/foreign resources and a replacement own lifetime cannot satisfy unresolved readback. Conclusive ordered readback retires this debt immediately, including while stopped or out of attempts; completed-cast telemetry does not bind future runs permanently to that lifetime. A later explicit run after reconnect still needs fresh current resources and all ordinary readiness, cooldown and allowance checks. Target HP readback is displayed separately and is never inferred from the healing amount. All candidates still need fresh HP.

The pending/uncertain owner blocks manual resource commands, other automation, travel/service acquisition and updater maintenance. A service request rejects before replacing field intent or starting its runtime, including after Stop, send failure or the ordinary confirmation deadline expires. It is runtime state and is never written to a profile. An unresolved owner from a departed lifetime remains held: timeout, page input and Stop do not prove server cancellation. The protocol has no request ID, so arbitrary identical late wire packets cannot be perfectly distinguished. No success or cancellation is claimed from a timer.

This policy targets players only. It does not chase, cast through maps, Heal monsters or model undead equipment on remote players, resurrect, buff, invite or otherwise manage a party. Remote elemental state is unavailable; the server remains responsible for ally/element validation and healing amount.
