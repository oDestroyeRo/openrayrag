# Observed party engagement

Combat's **Join monsters engaged by verified visible party members** option is disabled by default. Older settings and profiles may omit `automation.combat.partyEngagement`; omission and `false` keep outside engagements excluded. Only an explicit boolean `true` enables the acquisition exception. Unknown keys, null, strings and numeric options are rejected in TypeScript, native settings and profile imports. The Combat panel reports bounded verified/excluded engagement counts and exclusion reasons for the captured run policy.

A permission requires the shared `WorldState.partyActors` binding: a stable party member ID, a positive online entity ID, matching visible living player name and affiliation, and the current map, world and actor incarnation. Party entity zero is an offline/ambiguous sentinel, even if a visible actor has ID zero. Actual own actor zero remains valid. A self-inclusive party removal matching known own actor zero clears the entire association, following the official client's rule; an ambiguous offline-zero row cannot preserve or authorize stale ally bindings. Duplicate online IDs, missing or stale full affiliation evidence, replacement actors and map-only/resource-only rows cannot authorize an attacker. Authoritative observed zero HP also revokes a visible member's availability.

Opcode 11 Attack may establish participation by that verified player. A damaging targeted Skill result may do so only for a direct observed player source without a conflicting separate damage owner. Wire attacker `-1` means there is no separate damage owner; it does not mean the observed source is unknown. A separate attacker equal to the source is nonconflicting. A distinct attacker is excluded even if both players are party members. Unknown sources, indirect results and external standalone SkillImpact damage remain foreign.

Evidence is retained for each monster world/incarnation, with at most 150 monster records and eight party sources per monster. Unknown, outside, indirect, conflicting, revoked or overflowing claims are sticky for that monster lifetime. A later join or party hit cannot repair them. Membership removal, leave/logout, remapping, affiliation change, replacement, death or a replaced full association revokes the captured permission at the event boundary, before a same-tick rejoin can hide it. Monster replacement/removal and world reset retire the old record. Capacity overflow denies the exception instead of evicting a live foreign claim. The existing foreign-participation marker survives an unrecorded overflow: freeing a cache slot cannot turn that same monster lifetime into a clean party-only engagement. Only a replacement/removal or world reset retires that marker.

Revocation cancels only the affected automatic pursuit/attack/cast and retains requested run intent, session clocks and strategy attempt ledgers. A revoked sent cast suspends field execution while the controller retains its exact receipt; timeout alone cannot admit a new target. Existing accepted movement, unacknowledged approach and canceled-cast settlement fences remain in force; Stop prevents further continuation. Manual target commands keep their existing conservative foreign-target guard, and explicit manual skill owners do not inherit this automatic policy. Travel, recovery, emergency escape, maintenance, species/level/priority, radius, map/field, LOS and ammo admission remain under their existing owners and checks.

The shared observed-own-cast fence remains independent of that resource receipt. Unmarked proc-shaped results stay ambiguous; own StartWalk supplies availability only, and own ResetMotion opcode 111 supplies availability only for the observed self CounterAttack skill 31. Neither acknowledges the revoked skill request. Party invalidation follows the existing apply/cast/threat receive order. Threat escape still requires current own-world evidence, resource settlement and its captured quiet window; own death cancels an unsent preparation before automatic respawn without replaying the revoked attack.

This option changes automatic target eligibility only. Every outside participant still marks the monster foreign for own-kill and owned-drop attribution. Party-only or mixed kills cannot increment your kill counter or grant pickup under the default own-drop policy. Explicit all-drop pickup retains its separately configured scope. The feature never invites, accepts or messages a party.
