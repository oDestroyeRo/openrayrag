# Party leader follow and bounded rendezvous

In **Travel & follow**, choose **Player name** or **Current party leader**. Name mode preserves the existing visible-player behavior; a blank name disables it. Party mode clears the name and requires exactly one online leader with a positive entity ID, a stable member ID and a fresh shared actor/party binding. Offline rows, namesakes, duplicate online IDs and map or health updates cannot establish a replacement actor.

**Allow one bounded trip to the leader map per Start** is separately off by default. Enable it only when a fixed-portal rendezvous is wanted. The leader must first have been visibly verified. An authoritative full online row or leader-map update supplies destination map evidence for 15 seconds; unrelated health and traffic cannot refresh it. Map evidence supplies no coordinates. Unknown maps, denied destinations, missing fixed routes and destinations conflicting with an explicit field rectangle are unavailable. A forbidden origin permits departure under the captured policy, without permitting reentry.

The existing **Wait when player lost** allowance covers preparation, route planning, movement, every portal and destination visibility. The controller captures that original deadline and policy once. Leader-map changes cancel the trip instead of retargeting it. Repeated map updates cannot extend the deadline. Combat, pickup, supply, routines and manual owners wait for the captured trip; existing sent resource receipts block admission. Emergency safety can cancel follow while preserving its sent owners.

Actual leader movement keeps its current visible lifetime fresh without refreshing HP, SP, affiliation or destination-map evidence. An authoritative zero or unavailable HP observation prevents following and arrival completion until valid living evidence returns.

Expected portals require TravelController's fixed trigger/destination proof followed by a living own EnterServer spawn with matching name and ID. The server's OutOfSight own removal can preserve a captured final portal leg while the actual expected map and own arrival load; it cannot settle movement or authorize another owner. The destination also requires the same leader's fresh actual visible affiliation. A missing leader produces bounded waiting, never random search. Destination actor replacement cannot reuse the departure association. Successful following resumes the requested field run with its existing counters, time and death limits.

Party/leader loss, logout, identity remapping, unexpected map or Enter, reconnect, own replacement/death, Stop and timeout end the attempt. Stop does not prove that a sent walk or queued portal warp has settled: those receipts continue to block other owners until authoritative settlement or a fresh connection. After failure, use Stop and an explicit Start to retry. Automatic reconnect keeps field intent and budgets but disarms rendezvous. Profiles and form persistence contain policy only; runtime identities, association epochs and trips never persist.

Deterministic packet fixtures and the pinned Rebuild source establish these local contracts. Native UI, actual route traversal and actual party following are separate live evidence; they are not established by synthetic checks.
